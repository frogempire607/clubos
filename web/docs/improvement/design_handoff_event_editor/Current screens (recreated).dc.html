What's wrong today (from the source)

  • Payment is asked three separate times: “How registrants may pay”, “How registrants pay if approved”, and Bookings’ own “Payment method”.
  • Price is asked twice — the Pricing grid, then “Which price does the public link charge?”
  • Nothing is mutually exclusive: shared-cost split can be on while fixed prices are set; “charge on approve” can co-exist with “pay now by card”.
  • Participant category + coach approval only exist for Tournament; clinics and camps can’t have them.
  • Sessions carry no price — a 6-session camp is one $200 line.
  • Booking and EventRegistration are separate tables with separate screens: cancelling a booking leaves the invoice alive, and “Bookings” is never defined.
