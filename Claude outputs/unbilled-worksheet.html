Pulled 23 Sep 2026. Everyone who checked in during the last 90 days and holds no active membership (Paused people excluded). "Would owe" = months with a check-in in the last 90 days × the monthly price of the plan their usual class sits on. It is a guide, not an invoice.

The three choices
Comp — they train free on purpose. Billing centre → Edit → pick the plan, tick "Mark genuinely free ($0)" → Save; then "Already paid?" → their plan/option → amount 0 → Record $0 anyway; then profile → Current membership → Edit → Price 0 → Save; then billing centre → "Mark as deliberate comp". (Four steps today — this is the B13 panel's job to make one.) They count as Active, nothing is ever charged.
Plan — put them on a membership. Billing centre → Edit → plan, option, payment method → Save → Activate this setup now (saved card) or Already paid? (cash/check). Card path charges what the confirmation says; cash path records what you enter.
Gone — they're not coming back. Profile → Archive. Nothing is deleted; they drop off every count and can be un-archived.
