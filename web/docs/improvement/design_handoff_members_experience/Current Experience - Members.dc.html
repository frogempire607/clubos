Member Migration

Switch to AthletixOS without interrupting anyone's membership. Import your roster, send activation links, and members continue billing on their existing date.

Import / Migrate Members

Import all your clients

Upload your full roster in one CSV — active or not. Map any columns you have: contact info, address, guardian details, and your custom fields. Everyone comes in as a Prospect and is never charged until they activate.

Import / Migrate Members
2

Match memberships (separate file)

If your old system exports clients and memberships as separate files, upload the memberships CSV here. It matches each one (by email or name) to a client from step 1 and attaches their plan, price & billing date.

Match Memberships CSV

Before you switch — a few things to know

  • Don't cancel your old system until most members have activated. Recommended overlap: 2–4 weeks.
  • Imported members are never charged automatically.
  • Members are billed only after they add a payment method and their billing date arrives.
  • Billing continues on each member's existing date — not on import day.
